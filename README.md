# ADS PLUS+ — Strategic Marketing Consultancy

> **BUILT TO SELL+**  
> Strategy, creative and execution connected to one thing — your business objective.

Official web application for **Ads Plus+**, an editorial luxury, strategy-first marketing consultancy and creative agency established in Cairo in 2018, operating across Egypt, GCC markets (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar), and international corridors.

## ✦ Brand & Visual Direction

- **Dominant Aesthetic:** Black editorial luxury + bold typography + controlled red accents + cinematic imagery + intelligent motion.
- **Brand Colors:**
  - **Dominant Background:** Deep Pure Black (`#000000`)
  - **Primary Typography:** Crisp White (`#ffffff`)
  - **Ads Plus Red:** Interaction & Accent (`#ed1c24` / `#ff2b34`)
  - **Brand Warm Grey:** Secondary Surface & Copy (`#bab6ad` / `#837f77`)
- **Brand Devices:** The standalone `+` mark used intentionally as a recurring UX language (`Strategy +`, `Creative +`, `Performance +`, `Execution +`).
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: Aileron Heavy / Black (`Aileron-Heavy.ttf`) & SK Concretica (`SKConcretica-Trial.ttf`)
  - Body & Micro-copy: Aileron / Plus Jakarta Sans & Space Grotesk (`font-mono`)

## ✦ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Framework:** React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling:** Vanilla CSS Master Design System (`src/index.css`) with CSS custom properties, glassmorphism, and responsive breakpoints.
- **Icons & Micro-interactions:** Custom SVG brand icons (`src/components/Icons.jsx`) & Lucide React.
- **Media & 3D:** Authentic WebM 3D video renders, RGBA transparent client logos, and high-res photography.
- **SEO & Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5, Open Graph, Twitter Cards, and `schema.org/MarketingAgency` JSON-LD metadata.
