ADS PLUS+ — WEBSITE PACKAGE
===========================

1-Website
  The finished website as plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no frameworks).
  Open index.html in Chrome to view it, or upload the whole folder to any web host.
  Pages: index.html (Home), about.html, services.html, clients.html, contact.html
  assets/site.css  - all styles
  assets/site.js   - all interactions (intro, lights switch, scrolling story, doors menu,
                     spotlight, magnetic buttons, client wall, scrambling headings,
                     Cairo clock, logo easter egg)
  Before going live:
   - Office hours for the footer clock are placeholders (Sun–Thu, 10:00–18:00).
     Change them at the top of assets/site.js (OFFICE = ...).
   - The Contact page shows [YOUR EMAIL] and [YOUR WEBSITE]; replace them in contact.html.
   - The contact form is design only; connect it to a form service or your email.

2-Design-Source
  The editable design files from the Claude canvas (.dc.html + canvas.json).

3-Animations
  Every animation as GIF (social/WhatsApp) and MP4 (web): the 3D renders, the animated
  photos from the company profile, the office screens, red stairs, red door and phone scenes,
  and the opening intro.

4-3D-Interactive
  Live, rotatable 3D (three.js): the 5 service icons and the red glass plus.

5-Brand
  Company profile + brand identity PDF, and the brand guideline files
  (logos, icon, pattern, colour palette, Aileron and logo fonts).
  Note: the logo font is a TRIAL version — buy a licence before commercial use.

6-Extra-Images
  Stills used to build the site (photos from the profile, 3D stairs states, map, logo sprite).
